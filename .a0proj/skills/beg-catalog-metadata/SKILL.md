# beg-catalog-metadata — Label-Specific Skill

> **Scope:** Historical BEG (Beloved Entertainment Group) catalog archival. Documents 4 sub-imprints (Beloved Recordings, Yum Recordings, Updego Entertainment, Beloved Soundtracks), 30+ album releases from 1995-2002, and strategic partnerships with Dinemec Records and Crane Mountain Records.
> **Applies to:** Sync Licensing, A&R, Distribution & Digital Strategy (re-release scope, TBD)
> **Reference:** PRD §6.2 (Historical back-catalog — Beloved Entertainment Group)
> **Owner:** Wagner dos Santos (Principal) — WGNR Sounds Record Label

## Purpose

The Beloved Entertainment Group (BEG) is the **historical back-catalog** of WGNR Sounds (formerly operated as an independent label by Wagner dos Santos, NYC-based, November 1995 – January 2002). BEG produced 30+ domestic + international album releases across four sub-imprints. This skill preserves the canonical BEG catalog metadata for re-release scope, sync pitching, and historical reference.

## Sub-imprints (canonical)

| Sub-imprint | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Beloved Recordings** | Compilations |
| **Yum Recordings** | Rock |
| **Updego Entertainment** | Dance / Electronic / Club |
| **Beloved Soundtracks** | Film, television, Broadway |

**Breakthrough release:** "Ska: The Third Wave" compilation (1990s third-wave ska revival).

## Strategic Partnerships (historical)

- **Dinemec Records** (Switzerland)
- **Crane Mountain Records** (Boston)

## Historical Independent Artists (non-BEG sub-imprint)

- **Buzz Prophets**
- **Nerve**

## Legal Status (current)

BEG is currently filed as a **fictitious name under wgnr.ai, LLC** (per PRD §6.2, casing corrected from v1.1).

## Migration Path: DSP Re-distribution (TBD)

The historical catalog is documented but not currently on DSPs (open question §4 in PRD). When re-distribution activates:
1. Audit physical/digital originals to confirm master availability.
2. Route masters through the DistroKid sole-distributor pipeline (see skill `distrokid-delivery`).
3. Apply BEG sub-imprint label copy on the DSP release.
4. Coordinate with Sync Licensing for sync-friendly metadata (instrumentals, stems, alt mixes) where available.
