# ascap-publishing — Label-Specific Skill

> **Scope:** WGNR Sounds Music Publishing is the ASCAP-registered publishing sub-entity that administers performance rights for the catalog. Documents writer registration, ASCAP statement processing, and the master-rights-vs-publishing-rights distinction.
> **Applies to:** Legal & Business Affairs, Royalties & Finance, Sync Licensing
> **Reference:** PRD §4.5 (WGNR Sounds Music Publishing) + §7.5 (Legal & Business Affairs) + §7.7 (Royalties & Finance)
> **Owner:** Wagner dos Santos (Principal) — WGNR Sounds Record Label

## Purpose

**WGNR Sounds Music Publishing** is a separately-registered legal entity owned by WGNR Sounds. It is **ASCAP-registered** for performing rights royalty management and administers publishing rights for the catalog (active + historical BEG). This skill is the canonical reference for ASCAP registration, writer / publisher splits, and statement processing.

## Key Distinction: Master Rights vs. Publishing Rights

| Right type | Owner | Administrator | Revenue source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master rights** (the recording) | WGNR Sounds (the recording label) | WGNR Sounds | Label share from DSP streaming/sales |
| **Publishing rights** (the composition) | WGNR Sounds Music Publishing (ASCAP-registered) | WGNR Sounds Music Publishing | Performance rights royalties via ASCAP |

The recording label and the publishing entity are **distinct**. Master and publishing are separate rights, separate revenue streams, separate registrations.

## Writer Registration on Behalf of Catalog

WGNR Sounds Music Publishing registers writers on behalf of the catalog with ASCAP. For each release:
1. Identify writers (artist, co-writers, producer-writers).
2. Determine writer share vs. publisher share per the recording agreement.
3. Register the composition with ASCAP via WGNR Sounds Music Publishing's publisher account.

## Quarterly ASCAP Statement Processing

Performance rights royalties flow from ASCAP quarterly. WGNR Sounds Music Publishing receives statements and distributes per the splits on file.

**Current state:** ASCAP statements are insignificant today (Wagner is the sole artist performing under multiple names; royalties are not a day-to-day operation). The processing cadence activates when volumes grow or a publishing-administration partnership is signed (per PRD §7.7.1 + §7.9).

## Suno Licensing Note

For AI-generated works (DJ Farra, Sobralenses — 100% AI), Suno's commercial license terms apply to the master rights. Publishing rights for AI-generated compositions are subject to ASCAP's current AI-composition policy (verify at registration time).
