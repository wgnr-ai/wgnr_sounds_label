## What we BELIEVE we need, and why

### 1. Commission / work-made-for-hire agreement — Fiverr multi-instrumentalist

**Why we believe this:** our research indicates sound recordings can qualify as works made for hire for specially commissioned works with a signed writing (17 USC §101(2)), and we want the recorded instrumental performances to belong to the Principal from the outset. However, this citation comes from our own research — not counsel — and we understand the analysis may be more nuanced for independent contractors and sound recordings specifically.

**We ask the agent to determine the correct instrument** (WMFH agreement, assignment, license, or hybrid), taking into account: (a) Fiverr's default marketplace terms may conflict or add conditions; (b) the player is an independent contractor, not an employee; (c) the work is a sound recording.

**Elements we'd like covered:** parties; description of the commissioned recordings; consideration; ownership/vesting language (WMFH or the agent's preferred alternative); representation that the performance is original and non-infringing; a name/likeness/credit provision (the Principal prefers **no obligation of public credit**); electronic-signature validity.

### 2. Confirmatory assignment (or equivalent) for past collaborators

**Why:** the Principal has an extensive history of using collaborators on recordings (vocalists, players, engineers) **with no signed agreements**. We BELIEVE this means those collaborators own the copyright in their contributions, and the Principal holds only an implied, non-exclusive license — he can release the records, but cannot exclusively license, sell, or enforce those contributions alone.

**We ask the agent to determine the correct remediation instrument** (short confirmatory assignment, license conversion, or otherwise).

**Elements we'd like covered:** description of past contribution(s) by project/recording; consideration recital ("for good and valuable consideration, receipt acknowledged"); assignment (or equivalent) of the contribution's copyright; warranty of originality; an expansion clause if a contribution proves broader than described; electronic-signature clause; Florida choice of law.

### 3. Split-sheet / contribution-log template (optional — agent's judgment)

A one-page template to accompany future Velvut recordings: each collaborator, their contribution, its legal treatment (WMFH / assignment / license), and PRO/USCO registration implications — so unpapered collaborations never recur.

## Facts we verified this week (context for the agent — not legal conclusions)

- **USCO AI Report Part 2 (2025-01-29):** human authorship is required for copyrightability; fully AI-generated works are uncopyrightable; AI-assisted works are registrable for the human-authored portions, with AI-generated material disclosed and disclaimed at registration.
- **ASCAP/BMI/SOCAN joint policy (2025-10-28):** the PROs accept **partially** AI-generated compositions; fully AI works remain ineligible. Velvut songs qualify via the human layer (lyrics, vocals, arrangement direction).
- **Suno Premier plan:** commercial-use rights via assignment for outputs generated during an active paid subscription; **no retroactive rights** for pre-subscription free-tier outputs; terms wording shifted around the Warner deal ("you own" → "commercial use rights"). (suno.com ToS/Help Center, verified 2026-09-13.)
- **DistroKid:** disclosure-based AI policy; the Principal will answer "yes — AI instrumentals" on Suno-built tracks. **This is compliance metadata, not publicity — per Principal directive, AI use is never publicized.**
- **Raw Suno instrumental beds alone carry no copyright** — anyone may use them without license. The **released masters are protected** (human performances + production). We therefore believe anyone may use or re-create raw AI instrumentals freely, but may not use the Principal's released master without a master-use license from him.
- **Past collaborations with no signed paper:** we BELIEVE contributors own their contributions and the Principal holds only an implied non-exclusive license (release OK; exclusive licensing, sale, standalone enforcement — no).
