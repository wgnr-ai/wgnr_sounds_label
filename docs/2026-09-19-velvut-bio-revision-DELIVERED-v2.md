## Concept A — "Seemed like this day would never come" (LEAD)
**Word count:** 151 body words · 3 paragraphs ✓ (spec 150-250, 2-3)

He walked away from music for nearly three decades. Nobody made him. He just stopped. Wagner dos Santos was born and raised in New York City. At fifteen he recorded his first two demos, "Dust" and "Visions of You," pressed them to a 7-inch single, and handed them out himself. He sang in New Wave bands, fronted the alt-rock band Nerve, and spent years in the record business signing and producing other people: Kid Rock, Ron Wood, Roger Daltrey. Then he left. Settled in Orlando and got quiet.

The quiet didn't hold. Velvut is what came back out: nu-metal and alt-rock, moody verses that break into massive choruses, a deep baritone sitting right up front. The name comes from David Lynch's Blue Velvet, a film that unsettled him and stuck anyway. Velvut reads wrong for a half-second before it reads right. That's the idea.

New music coming soon on WGNR Sounds.

## Concept B — "Velvut" (ALTERNATE)
**Word count:** 158 body words · 3 paragraphs ✓ (spec 150-250, 2-3)

Velvut. If it reads wrong for a half-second before it reads right, that's the idea. The band is fronted by Wagner dos Santos, a deep baritone from New York City. The music is nu-metal and alt-rock: moody, atmospheric verses that open into massive choruses. The name came from David Lynch's Blue Velvet, one of the most unsettling films he'd ever seen, and it resonated anyway. He wanted this project to work the same way. Unsettling and infectious at the same time.

He's done this before. At fifteen he recorded his first two demos, "Dust" and "Visions of You," pressed to a 7-inch and handed out by hand in New York. Frontman of the alt-rock band Nerve in the nineties. Years in A&R and production, including records with Kid Rock, Ron Wood, and Roger Daltrey. Then nearly three decades away from music, based in Orlando.

He's back. The voice never went anywhere. New music coming soon on WGNR Sounds.
