Velvut. If it reads wrong for a half-second before it reads right, that's the idea. The band is fronted by Wagner dos Santos, former frontman and lead singer of the New York City rock bands Buzz Prophets and Nerve. The name came from David Lynch's Blue Velvet, one of the most unsettling films he'd ever seen, and it resonated anyway. He wanted this project to work the same way. Unsettling and infectious at the same time. The music is nu-metal and alt-rock: moody, atmospheric verses that open into massive choruses.

He was fifteen when he recorded his first two demos, "Dust" and "Visions of You." At seventeen he went back into the studio and recorded them again, plus a new song, "The Third Day of Sorrow," and a dance remix of it, with producer Rob "Bung" Bengston. The recordings were commercially released as his debut vinyl EP and distributed nationally by IRD (RED Distribution). Then came Buzz Prophets, then Nerve, and then nearly three decades of quiet. Until now.
