## 2. What the Bio Must Contain (facts — locked, no invention)

Every claim below is Principal-declared and citable. Anything not listed is UNVERIFIED — do not add biographical claims of your own.

**The band:**
- Velvut: explosive nu-metal and alt-rock. Moody, atmospheric verses → massive anthemic choruses. Minor key, deep bass, full kit, upfront deep baritone.
- Frontman and lead singer: **Wagner dos Santos** (formerly recorded under the artist name "Wágner" — accent dropped; mention only if it earns its place).

**The name origin:**
- Named for David Lynch's *Blue Velvet* — among the most unsettling films the frontman has ever seen, and it resonated anyway, emotionally and artistically. He wanted the project to be unsettling and infectious at the same time. "Velvet" was taken, so the spelling bent: Velvut.

**The frontman's history (newly declared — this is the material the Principal wants worked in):**
- Born and raised in New York City.
- At 15: recorded first two demos, "Dust" and "Visions of You" — pressed to a 7" single, hand-distributed.
- Shaped by the New Romantics and the mid-eighties New Wave movement.
- Former frontman of the mid-nineties alternative rock band **Nerve**.
- Founded **Novus Records**; A&R career: Imago, TVT, Continuum (signed/produced Kid Rock, Ron Wood, Roger Daltrey), then Beloved Entertainment Group ("Ska: The Third Wave" compilation).
- Moved to Orlando, FL in the late nineties; stepped away from music for nearly three decades; now returned as a recording artist.
- Label: **WGNR Sounds, a division of wgnr.ai**.

**Ruled out (do not include):** Mauricio Mirapalheta (collaborator), studio trivia (The Workshoppe, Cy Curnin, Victor Loud), RED distribution history. The Principal judged these 1990s trivia that dilute the story — but you may argue otherwise if you think one of them is the missing ingredient.

## 5. Voice Constraints (binding)

- **Plain talk.** The artist's own lyric style is conversational, concrete, no decoration. The bio should feel like the same person. If a line reads like a publicist wrote it ("stands as a rare…"), think twice.

- **The turn:** one sharp plain-word line beats a paragraph of imagery.
- Calm delivery. The subject burns; the prose shouldn't.
