Velvut. If it reads wrong for a half-second before it reads right, that's the idea. The band is fronted by Wagner dos Santos, former frontman and lead singer of the New York City rock bands Buzz Prophets and Nerve. The music is nu-metal and alt-rock: moody, atmospheric verses that open into massive choruses. The name came from David Lynch's Blue Velvet, one of the most unsettling films he'd ever seen, and it resonated anyway. He wanted this project to work the same way. Unsettling and infectious at the same time.

He was fifteen when he recorded his first two demos, "Dust" and "Visions of You." At seventeen he went back into the studio and recorded them again, plus a new song, "The Third Day of Sorrow," and a dance remix of it, at a legendary recording studio with a producer he trusted. The recordings were pressed to vinyl EPs and distributed nationally by Important Record Distributors. Then came Buzz Prophets, then Nerve. Then nearly three decades of quiet, based in Orlando.

He's back. The voice never went anywhere. New music coming soon on WGNR Sounds.
